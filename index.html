About the institute

NIST University was founded to do one thing well: teach engineering as a practical craft, not just a set of exams to clear. Our classrooms sit next to our labs, and most courses spend as much time on the workbench as at the whiteboard.

Over three decades, the institute has grown from a single diploma programme into four full degree courses, run by faculty who mostly came up through industry before they came up through academia. Class sizes stay small enough that professors know your name by the second week.

Our Mission

To give every student the technical grounding, the hands-on practice and the confidence to solve real engineering problems from day one of their career.

Our Vision

To be recognised as a college where graduates are known for what they can build, not just what they can recite.
